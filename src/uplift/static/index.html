Uplift Campaign Planner

Who should get the SMS? The model estimates how much contacting each client changes the chance they buy, then picks the contact list that maximises profit for your costs. Clients who would buy anyway are not worth a coupon.

Clients

Synthetic clients are drawn from aggregated training statistics, not real customers. CSV columns: see /docs or examples/clients_example.csv in the repository.
